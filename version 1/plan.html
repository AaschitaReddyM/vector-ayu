Buildathon Dallas 2026 — Plan of Action

Enterprise Product Specification, Defensive Engineering Matrix, Demo Narrative & Computational Architecture for VAYU Layer

The VAYU Layer is an enterprise B2B predictive analytics middleware that connects real-time environmental data with hospital EHR records to predict acute clinical emergencies in chronic patients 24–72 hours before they occur. It targets Value-Based Care organizations where avoidable ER visits create steep financial penalties.

🫁 Respiratory — COPD / Asthma ❤️ Cardiovascular — CHF / AFib / Stroke 🩸 Metabolic — Diabetes / CKD
⚡ Important Buildathon Dallas 2026 is June 18–19, 2026 (32-hour live event). The spec prescribes a 90/10 split: 90% pre-built locally, 10% live cloud infra at the event. Three chronic disease vectors are targeted across multi-task AI prediction heads.

🧩 Core Product Components

Five integrated modules form the VAYU platform — each designed for operational realism within Value-Based Care workflows.

📊
1. Provider Dashboard
Real-time care coordination interface showing high-risk patients during environmental events. Displays 72-hour triage queues, H3 hex maps, SHAP explainability panels, and climate volatility deltas — all embedded via SMART on FHIR inside Epic/Cerner.
🧠
2. Patient Risk Engine
Dynamic risk scoring that fuses environmental signals (AQI, heat index, wildfire smoke) with clinical factors (condition severity, medications, age, smoking status, prior ER utilization) through a multi-task Temporal Fusion Transformer with three prediction heads.
💬
3. AI Outreach Generator
Multilingual, empathetic preventative messaging powered by MindStudio AI Worker. Generates personalized SMS campaigns with condition-specific guidance, translated into patient-preferred languages with culturally sensitive framing and ambient health-weather context.
✅
4. Intervention Approval Workflow
End-to-end clinical approval pipeline: Flagged → Generated → Approved → Scheduled → Logged. 48-hour "Approve & Release" single-click workflow ensures clinician oversight before any patient-facing communication is dispatched.
🌍
5. Environmental Intelligence Layer
Dallas-focused AQI monitoring, real-time weather feeds, heat index calculations, and wildfire smoke overlays. Data ingested via streaming pipeline, indexed to Uber H3 Resolution 7 hexagonal spatial cells, and fused with Exposure Attenuation models (Effective Exposure = Outdoor Hazard × (1 − Shielding Coefficient)) for neighborhood-level risk assessment.

🔐 B2B Population Consent Architecture

Dual-track consent model ensures VAYU operates legally across 100% of the patient population — not just the minority who opt in. Track B leverages HIPAA "Healthcare Operations" provisions to provide coverage without explicit patient consent.

🟢 Track A — Fully Consented

Automated SMS Pipeline

  • Patient provides explicit consent for automated outreach
  • Full H3 hexagonal location tracking for precise spatial risk
  • Wearable vitals ingestion (SpO₂, heart rate, glucose)
  • 48-hour AI-generated SMS campaigns with approval workflow
  • Consumer app access with VAYU Index scoring
  • Rich SHAP explainability views available to patient
🟡 Track B — Non-Consented

Internal Triage Only

  • No patient-facing communications or app access
  • Billing ZIP code mapping (not precise H3 location)
  • Internal provider dashboard risk visibility only
  • Manual phone calls by clinical staff when flagged
  • HIPAA "Healthcare Operations" legal provision
  • Graceful degradation — EHR + climate data only

🎬 Demo Script — Buildathon Narrative

A structured 5-minute pitch sequence designed to tell a compelling story from problem to vision.

1
Opening — The Problem Statement
"Healthcare reacts AFTER crises happen." — Every year, millions of chronic disease patients end up in emergency rooms for events that were clinically predictable days in advance. The healthcare system has no early warning infrastructure.
2
The Hidden Driver
"Climate events silently worsen chronic disease before hospitals can respond." — AQI spikes, heat domes, wildfire smoke, and pressure drops trigger predictable cascades of COPD exacerbations, heart failure decompensations, and diabetic crises. This is invisible to current clinical tools.
3
The Solution
"VAYU predicts which patients are likely to deteriorate 48–72 hours before emergency escalation." — We are an enterprise middleware layer that fuses real-time environmental intelligence with EHR clinical data to give healthcare organizations predictive triage capabilities they've never had.
4
Live Demo Walkthrough
Show a Dallas AQI spike event triggering automatic patient reprioritization → the provider dashboard updating risk scores in real time → AI-generated outreach messages appearing in the approval queue → single-click "Approve & Release" dispatching multilingual SMS campaigns to consented patients.
5
Business Model
Medicare Advantage plans, ACOs, and risk-bearing healthcare groups lose money when patients hit the ER. VAYU is a PMPM enterprise SaaS — per-member-per-month pricing — that pays for itself by preventing a single avoided hospitalization per hundred patients.
🌍
Closing Vision
"We are building the climate intelligence layer for healthcare operations." — Every health system in the country will need this as climate volatility increases. VAYU is the infrastructure that makes proactive, climate-aware care coordination possible at scale.

🎯 Five Most Important Rules

Non-negotiable principles from the V1 spec that guide every build decision. Violating any one of these risks demo failure.

1
Polished UI > Sophisticated Backend
Judges evaluate what they can see. A stunning, pixel-perfect interface with hardcoded data will always beat a technically brilliant backend with an ugly frontend. Invest 70% of effort into the visual experience.
2
Operational Realism > Technical Complexity
Demonstrate that the product could actually work in a hospital — consent flows, approval workflows, provider notifications. Real clinical operations, not academic ML pipelines.
3
One Disease Workflow Only (For Demo)
Do not try to demo all three disease vectors simultaneously. Pick Respiratory (COPD/Asthma + AQI) as the primary demo path and make it flawless. The architecture supports all three, but the demo shows one done perfectly.
4
Focus on the Demo Experience
Every feature must serve the 5-minute demo narrative. If a feature doesn't appear in the demo flow, it doesn't exist. Ruthlessly cut scope to protect the critical path.
5
Build the Story, Not the Infrastructure
You are not building production infrastructure in 32 hours. You are building a compelling, visually stunning demonstration that tells the story of a product that could change healthcare. The narrative IS the product.
